Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and how it's built to be used responsibly.

Privacy overview

MyFirstBank doesn't track who you are.

What's collected: anonymous, aggregate visit data via GoatCounter — page views, which filters get used, how many visitors reach Ask the Agent. No cookies, no personal identifiers, nothing tied to an individual visitor.

What's never collected or stored: your name, email, SSN, ITIN, account numbers, or any other personal identifier. Ask the Agent doesn't save your chat history beyond the live browser session — refresh the page and it's gone. We never ask for personal identifiers, and if you paste one into a question anyway, the agent is built to skip over it rather than repeat it back.

Third parties

Two outside services touch this site:

Anthropic — Ask the Agent's questions are processed through Anthropic's API (via a server-side proxy that keeps our API key private) to generate responses. Your question text is sent to Anthropic and handled under Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms — we don't control that processing. Don't type anything personally identifying into a chat question; the agent doesn't need it to answer.

GoatCounter — a cookieless, privacy-focused analytics service. It counts visits and page views in aggregate; it can't identify individual visitors and doesn't use tracking cookies.

Accessibility statement

MyFirstBank is built to work for everyone. That includes WCAG AA contrast on every color used for meaning (not just decoration), full keyboard navigation with visible focus states across the whole site — navbar, filters, cards, the Compare table, and the chat — and respecting your device's reduced-motion setting everywhere animation appears, from page transitions to flip cards to the chat's typing indicator.

If you hit an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, let us know — see Contact below.
